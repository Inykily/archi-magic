Tune material colors, then apply them in one flow

Pick a color by eye for each material. The page cannot talk to Revit: click Copy changes for Revit, paste the text back in chat, and the agent shows a before/after preview, runs a dry run, and asks you to click Keep or Discard in Revit.

Palette helper: light greys that stay readable

Pick a material, ask for N light greys, and click one to use it as that material's shading. Each grey states its WCAG contrast ratio against black text and against the material's pattern color. Greys stop at the darkest grey that still keeps black text at 4.5:1 (WCAG AA).
